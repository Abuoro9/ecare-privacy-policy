Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how E-Care collects, uses, and protects your information.

About E-Care

E-Care is designed to help users quickly connect with the nearest hospital or hospitals of their choosing with the press of a button. E-Care does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and does not replace professional healthcare services.

Information Collection

E-Care does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless explicitly stated. Location data is used only to identify nearby hospitals and is not stored or shared.

Permissions

E-Care may request permissions such as location, phone access, and internet connectivity solely to enable core app functionality. These permissions are not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

Subscriptions and Payments

E-Care offers optional paid subscriptions that provide access to premium features. Subscriptions are billed on a monthly basis and automatically renew unless cancelled according to the applicable Google Play subscription terms.

All payments are processed by third-party payment providers, and E-Care does not collect or store payment or billing information.

Third-Party Services

E-Care may use third-party services such as analytics or advertising providers to improve app performance and sustainability. These services may collect limited information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
